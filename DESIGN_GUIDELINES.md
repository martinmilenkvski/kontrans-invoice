# KONTRANS Kinetic Design Guidelines

A brief overview of the "Kinetic Swiss-Brutalist" aesthetic used for the KONTRANS digital identity.

## 1. Core Visual Identity
- **Aesthetic**: Swiss-Brutalist — prioritized by high-contrast typography, rigid grid structures, and "engineered" layout modules.
- **Thematic Tone**: Operational Excellence & Kinetic Precision.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Background**: `#080808` (Rich Black)
- **Primary Foreground**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Brand Accent**: `#D42B2B` (Industrial Red) — used exclusively for emphasis, CTAs, and technical status indicators.
- **Secondary Surfaces**: White with very low opacity (`white/5`, `white/10`) for borders and dividers.

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font**: **Sans-serif (Jost)**. Used for headlines and body.
  - Headlines: Heavy weights (`font-black`), lowercase or uppercase with tight tracking.
  - Body: Medium weight, generous line-height for readability.
- **Technical Labels**: **Monospaced**. Used for indices (`001 // LABEL`) and telemetry data.
  - Styling: Wide tracking (`tracking-[0.3em]` to `tracking-[0.5em]`), small font sizes (10-12px), full uppercase.

## 4. Interaction Patterns (Kinetic)
- **CTA Buttons**: 
  - Dual-layer hover: White background by default, red background slides in from the side/bottom on hover.
  - Text: Color should flip (e.g., Black to White) to maintain high contrast.
- **Metrics/Stats**: 
  - "Slot-machine" digit rolling animations for numerical data.
  - Bordered "HUD" modules for metric grouping.
- **Links**: 
  - Minimalist text-based links with kinetic underlines that expand or change color on hover.

## 5. Layout & Spacing
- **Container**: Standardized at `max-w-[1600px]` with horizontal padding (`px-4`).
- **Dividers**: Prefer `divide-y divide-white/10` or subtle `1px` lines over margin gaps.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Large section padding (`py-20` to `py-40`) to create specialized "editorial" spreads.

## 6. Imagery
- **Quality**: Cinematic, high-resolution photography.
- **Treatment**: High contrast, subtle desaturation, or dark environmental-lighting to match the `#080808` background.
